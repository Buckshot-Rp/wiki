# Footage

## Reports

A player report needs **bodycam** — your recorded gameplay with game audio and mic. Staff will not run a he-said scene without it.

Leave the clip off only if the report is a **cheater** or a **tip** (something you want staff to look at, not a fight over a rule). How to file a cheater report is under [Cheaters](#cheaters).

Hold **F5** for player IDs and put them in the ticket. The clip should be the whole scene: start, the break, the end. Unedited. Sound on.

Only someone **in** the scene opens that ticket. You can use another person’s clip. You cannot open it for a friend.

You can talk to the other player first if you do not want to escalate.

Staff will not tell you the outcome or pass around evidence. Ticket contents stay in the ticket. How to behave in a ticket is on [Community](/rules/community#tickets).

Scene reports go in within **48 hours**. Harassment, hate, and other serious conduct have no clock — ticket those now. Cheaters are the same. See [Cheaters](#cheaters).

## Cheaters

They get banned. Do not argue with them in chat, call them out in the city, or feed the show. Open a ticket.

Tell us what they were doing and, if you can, **when**. Approximate times are enough. Staff can pull logs, see how the exploit ran, and patch the server so it is harder next time.

A clip still helps if you have one. It is not required for this kind of report.

## Always record

Record the session. Keep clips **7 days** after anything that might get reported.

If staff ask for footage of a kill or a scene and you cannot produce it, that is a **2 week** ban. If you are in fights or a war a lot and you cannot produce POV when staff ask, that weighs against you.

No edited, cut, or fake clips. Tampering is a perm.

NVIDIA Shadowplay or Medal is fine.

## In character

You cannot use a recording to dodge [NLR](/rules/character#new-life-rule) or to get revenge after a death.

A recording only counts in character if a visible in-game camera is rolling (phone, camera). Do not say you “have it clipped” unless that camera is actually out.

Businesses may use CCTV **inside** the business. Only employees see that CCTV. Civilians do not have dash cams or bodycams.

Only **PD and EMS** wear in-game bodycams.

## CCTV for investigations

PD and authorized investigators can pull footage from scripted cameras when the scene supports it — not as the only evidence, and not without in-city work first.

| Location type | Footage window |
|---|---|
| Houses, stores, banks (general) | **45 minutes** after the scene |
| PD, prison, player-owned businesses | **1 hour** after the scene |

Transaction records from covered cameras can be pulled the **same day** if the scene happened in those areas. Supportive info only — build the case in the city first.
