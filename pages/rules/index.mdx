# Rules

Connecting to Buckshot Discord or the city means you are **18+**, you have read this wiki, and you will follow it.

These pages are the line. They will not fit every situation, and they are not a script you pause the city to quote. Staff look at the **impact**. The goal is to protect the player experience and keep this a safe, enjoyable space — not to police every choice.

If a situation led to a good scene for everyone in it, there may be nothing to step in on. Losing a gunfight or a chase is not a punishment. Play it. If something broke, [report it](/rules/footage) after the scene is done. Do not go OOC to argue rules in the middle of RP.

If you think a rule sucks, say so. [Feedback](/rules/feedback) is how we talk about it. We would rather hear that than watch someone pick the wording apart to squeeze through a gap.

<Callout type="warning">
  Not knowing a rule is not a defense. Examples are examples — not the only way to break something. Staff can act without a report, and can act on something that is not listed. **Bans are always staff’s call**, even when you think the wiki covers you. Do not pick the wording apart to squeeze in or out of a line — we will not entertain that. Reviews use more than one staff opinion. Rules can change; staff will say so in Discord. [Warning points](/rules/community#warning-points) still fire on their own — staff cannot waive those thresholds.
</Callout>

Discord and in-game both count. Speak **English** on both.

If you see a break, [report it](/rules/footage) with player IDs (**F5** shows them). You can talk to the other player first if you do not want a ticket.

Staff would rather teach than hammer an honest mistake. Some lines are still an instant or perm ban.

Open a ticket, then stay in it. A ticket with no activity for **24 hours** gets closed. Staff will not write you an essay about why. Tickets are not a camping spot. They take time out of someone’s day, and there is no reason to hold that if you are not participating. See [Tickets](/rules/community#tickets).
