# Character

Read this before you make one.

## Names and concept

Use a real-person name. Original character. No vulgar puns, no celebrity names, no cartoon or series names, no meme names, no content-creator names.

Bad: Ben Dover, Steve Harvey, Looney Tunes, Chicken Little. If staff make you change it, open a ticket.

Do not copy another player’s character, backstory, or ongoing storyline. Bring your own concept. Copycat RP gets a change-or-delete request with no refund.

The character is **18+**. No pregnancy RP. No child RP. No non-human RP unless staff signed off in a ticket.

## Alts and circles

Each character is its own person. They do not share memories, relationships, or backstory unless that was roleplayed. Two of your characters cannot sit in the same friend group or gang.

Do not move cash, items, or property between your characters, including through a “friend.” Staff will delete it.

Do not make and delete characters to farm starter cash. Staff can see it. That is a ban.

To play a different character, log out and fly back in. Do not swap mid-session in the city.

## Starter clothes

Change your look after spawn. Do not walk the city in default clothes unless you are going straight to a clothing store or barber.

Look like a person. Arena / meme kits meant to mock the city are out (bowl cut, cat ears, surgical gloves, and goggles as a bit). Costumes are fine. A whole identity built to wreck immersion is not.

Fresh characters are not a robbery target. See [Fresh spawns](/rules/robbery#fresh-spawns).

## Value of life

Your life is not a joke. Fear getting hurt, killed, or locked up.

If you are held up or unarmed, you surrender. The only exception is if your gun is already out and the threat is right in front of you. That also covers people your character would care about — friends, hostages, civilians in your care.

You can value your freedom. Running from PD is allowed. Jumping off a roof to escape, walking into traffic to stop a car, or drawing when guns are already on you is not. You are not John Wick.

Fear of PD and lawful orders live under [Roleplay](/rules/roleplay#low-effort-rp). Shooting someone without a story is [RDM](/rules/combat#random-deathmatch).

### Fear RP

When you are outnumbered **2:1** with **weapons drawn** against you, comply or accept the risk. Only people showing a gun count — four people with one gun out is not 4v2. Law enforcement follows department escalation; this line is for civs and criminals.

If you are already in your car and someone pulls a gun, speeding away is at your own risk.

If they tell you not to touch radio, phone, or panic — comply or die.

## Injuries

Play them. If you were shot, you do not sprint back into the same fight. After EMS treats you, stay injured for a while — limp a broken leg, do not immediately hunt revenge.

### Downed vs out

This is **how you play while down** — posture and what you can say. [New life rule](#new-life-rule) is **what you remember afterward**.

**Downed** (on your side): hurt but conscious. You can speak and use radio. You remember what led here. Act injured.

**Out** (on your back): you are out for the scene. No radio, no calling your set, no trash talk or debrief. You **can** talk to EMS or PD treating you — answer questions, `/me` injuries, cooperate with inspect. Head shots, extreme falls, and similar — play **out**, not chatty on your side.

If EMS revives you before you fully bleed out, memory loss may not apply the same way. If you bleed out first, the hazy-memory line in NLR does.

### Medical custody

After EMS revives you, you are in **medical custody** until they clear you. No sprinting into revenge, no leaving mid-treatment, no running a chase on freshly patched legs. Play hurt until they say you are good.

If EMS is on duty and can reach you, use them — do not skip to NPC or AI ambulance. AI ambulance is fine if EMS is not available, or you have waited out the whole timer and requested assistance. Another civ or cop can transport you if EMS is tied up elsewhere; using that to dodge medical RP is a ticket.

Do not log out to skip EMS, the wait, or a revive fee. After a scene, wait **5 minutes** from the last interaction before you respawn if EMS never showed — see [Respawn](#respawn).

PD who were hit in the vest can say so while downed and stay on duty if that is the truth.

## Respawn

Last resort. Do not respawn unless you are unreachable by PD or EMS and you have waited a reasonable time, or you are stuck in a game-breaking glitch.

If you are in a gang fight or any live scene, use the medical alert and wait. Respawning in the middle of that is out.

If you are glitched and PD is already on you, turn yourself in. Do not respawn out of it.

Respawning to dodge a search, a loot, or a hold-up is an automatic ban.

Do not follow someone to the hospital after they respawn to keep the fight going.

## Coma

A coma is not a perma. Stay at the hospital on that character and play it. If you went down in the middle of a PD scene to dodge a sentence, you still serve it when you wake up.

## New life rule

What you remember depends on how you went down.

- **Knockout (melee):** you remember it.
- **Critical (gunshot or a real injury):** fuzzy. You might recall the talk or a vague face, not a full ID.
- **Bleed out:** hazy, unreliable scraps. Nothing you can act on like a fact.
- **Respawn:** nothing. You do not remember what led to going down. You cannot hunt the people who dropped you.

You may act on it only if you were taken to hospital, or EMS / PD treated you. A witness can tell you what happened in character and refresh that memory.

After a death, stay away from who killed you for **30 minutes**. Do not bring the scene back up unless someone reminds you in character.

NLR applies to groups. If your set gets spun and everyone goes down, none of you know exactly who did it or how. Do not debrief on radio while **out** — see [Downed vs out](#downed-vs-out).

## Suicide RP

Not allowed. See [Graphic content](/rules/roleplay#graphic-content).

## Character kill

You cannot CK another player. Nobody can force or push a perma onto you.

If you want to CK **your** character, delete it. That choice is yours. Items, skills, cash, property, and anything else on that character stay dead with them. Nothing transfers to another character. The new one does not remember phone numbers, friends, or the old life. Roleplay your way back in.
