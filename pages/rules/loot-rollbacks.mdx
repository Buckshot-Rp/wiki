# Loot rollbacks

If an item is pulled from the city, it is gone. Staff do not need to check in with you or approve it first.

Tebex items, and items you fairly earned in game, are not on that list. Those stay yours.

## Vehicles that should not exist

Sometimes a car slips through: weaponized, listed by mistake, priced so far off it is obvious. Staff can pull it.

If that happens, you have **24 hours** to take a refund or get a replacement from a dealership. After it is gone, everything that came with it is gone too — including the trunk. That is not [lost loot](/rules/bug-reports#lost-loot).

If you already know it is wrong, do not buy it. You will not get it back, and you will not get a refund.
