# Combat

## Group size

**5** is the cap for any criminal or hostile scene. Not just gangs. Civs, a mixed crew from two sets, friends on a store, a kidnapping, a chase, an airdrop. Two from one group and three from another is still **5**.

Lookouts, drivers, and scouts count. Hostages do not.

Only those five are in it. Nobody else on radio, staging cars, or grabbing bodies before the scene is over. You can have more people in a radio channel. They do not talk this scene.

Police numbers do not raise it. Swapping people mid-scene is out.

If PD is chasing you, you cannot run back to a block with more than five waiting to ambush. If you get hit on your turf, manage the numbers. Do not treat it as a free-for-all past **5**.

If another group rolls up and opens fire on your street, the chase is still **5**. No extras.

One heist at a time. Do not split the set and hit two jobs at once.

Gang-only extras live under [Gangs](/rules/gangs).

## Random deathmatch

You do not shoot or kill without a story and an initiation. See [Value of life](/rules/character#value-of-life). Initiation is a conversation, not one line. They need a real chance to answer before you go to guns.

Shooting from a car still needs that initiation. A drive-by with no words is RDM. Gang wars still follow [Gangs](/rules/gangs#gang-wars) — you still say something first.

Do not kill someone for being somewhere a **legal job** put them (trucking, fishing, hunting). The only exception is a scripted fight event such as an airdrop — **5** per group, and what happens there stays there.

If a shootout goes quiet for **over 5 minutes** and both sides left, you reinitiate. Wait **20 minutes** before you start another shootout. Stacking big casualty shootouts with no cooldown is fail RP.

Bad reasons:

- One-liners (“what those colors mean,” “hey buddy,” “stay off our block,” “ABC did it to you”)
- They annoyed you
- A car accident
- They refused you service
- They are on a legal job
- Their shirt or car is your gang color (car-color kills are a **perm**)

Good reasons:

- Repeated disrespect that actually built up
- A known rival on your turf during an active beef, poking you
- A snitch with in-character proof (bodycam, wire)
- Payback for a recent shooting, robbery, or ambush
- Someone interfering with a trap, drop, or other illegal work
- They threatened serious harm and you come back with a planned hit
- A kidnapping meant to scare a rival
- A rival poking you across several scenes with real buildup

## Vehicular deathmatch

Do not use a vehicle as a weapon. One accident is RP — insurance, words, payback. Running someone over until they are dead is a ticket. Ramming to strip a car is [unrealistic driving](#unrealistic-driving).

One tap with the car is only fine if it is the only realistic way out and staying would get you killed. That is not a staged execution. Only a live or just-started fight.

Do not warlord. Ramming, tackle spam, or taser to park someone so you can execute them while they cannot move is out.

## Unrealistic driving

Drive like a person who wants to live. Stunt jumps on a getaway are fine. These are not:

- Off a pier or into the ocean
- Head-on into other cars like a missile
- Off-road or up a mountain in something that does not belong there
- Ramming hard enough to maim or kill
- Using the car to strip other cars on purpose
- Off a high drop on a bike or car
- Stashing the car in a garage or jumping a locked interior mid-chase

## Hostages and kidnapping

High-quality RP. No fake hostages (friends or set members). No NPC hostages. Do not milk the same business for hostages on a loop.

Cap a hostage at **45 minutes** if the scene is not still moving. You cannot walk the hostage off the scene as a human shield.

**EMS is never a hostage.** Not in any scene. Robbing or killing EMS is [Robbery](/rules/robbery#ems) — only if they started it with you.

Store and heist waits, and grabbing PD, live under [Robbery](/rules/robbery).

## Bodies

Do not dump people in water, off cliffs, or anywhere EMS cannot work. That is fail RP, not a flex. Dumping a car in the ocean with no RP is the same bucket. Ocean dumps that wipe memory live under [Gangs](/rules/gangs#ocean-dumps).

## After a shootout

Loot for **2 minutes**, or until PD starts cleanup — whichever comes first. Do not make people wait while you loot. Staying on scene with several cops already there is fail RP.

Conflict ends after **5 minutes** with no shooting. After that you do not loot, restock, and run it back. Pulling a downed member out has to happen before that clock. After that it is a new fight.

## Endless conflict

Every fight needs a way out. Running the same harm into the ground with no attempt to end it is not valuing your life. Hunting a group or person with no realistic off-ramp is griefing.

## Convoys

**3** vehicles in a rolling shootout. PD follows their own process. If your car dies in the fight, stealing another to leave is fine. Roaming the map in a pack of supers to hunt people is fail RP.

## While you are down

You are still in character. Do not trash talk, complain about scripts, or go AFK unless you are unconscious. If someone is treating you, answer in character. Issues go in a ticket, not into the scene.
