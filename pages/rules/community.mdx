# Community

These apply in Discord and in-game.

## Respect

No impatience, arguing, or talking down to people. Different views are fine — disagree without turning it into a fight.

Staff and members are volunteers. They work at their pace, not yours. Do not spam Discord or OOC. Do not DM developers. Do not pull staff aside in the city to “talk” — open a ticket or `/report`.

Do not troll players, PD, or staff. Do not witch-hunt. Seeing a cheater is a [ticket](/rules/footage#cheaters), not a public pile-on. Walking the city to complain is not [feedback](/rules/feedback) — that is a ticket, or it is a problem.

## Harassment and discrimination

No bullying, harassment, stalking, or predatory behavior. No slurs or targeting someone for age, gender, race, disability, orientation, or religion. Impersonation, leaking DMs, doxxing, and defamation count.

This is IC and OOC. “It is just RP” is not a defense.

Do not change a Steam, Discord, or in-game name to look like staff. Do not claim to be staff.

Strong language is fine if it is not aimed at a person. “Pineapple on pizza tastes like shit” is fine. “You’re a piece of shit for liking it” is not.

Harassment is aimed at the **player**, not the character. That includes:

- Sexual harassment of any kind — see [Sexual RP](/rules/roleplay#sexual-rp)
- DMing or going OOC after they told you to stop
- Unsolicited romantic or sexual DMs to someone you met through RP
- Sustained intimidation meant to drive them off the city

Aggressive RP between characters is not harassment. You cannot use this line to dodge what your character earned.

If it is happening to you, do not play along. Open a ticket.

## Real-world politics and identity RP

Do not roleplay discrimination or politics built on real-world identities. Racism, homophobia, transphobia, sexism, and real-world political issues are out — whichever “side” you think you are playing.

You cannot play the victim of those things, accuse another character of them, or use them as a plot. Bias toward a fictional gang, town, faction, or job is normal RP.

If someone else starts it, do not engage in character. Ticket it.

## Toxicity

Do not make the city hostile on purpose. OOC pile-ons, griefing with no scene, spreading lies about staff or the city, or trying to trash the community’s name are out.

[Feedback](/rules/feedback) is the place for criticism. Public attacks are not.

## NSFW

No explicit images, text, avatars, banners, or links on Discord, in-game, or city socials. Eighteen-plus does not mean people want porn or gore in the room.

## Advertising

Do not advertise products, services, pages, or other servers here — public or in DMs. Staff can allow an exception. Paid help for in-game benefits is [real-world trading](/rules/server#real-world-trading).

Clips and streams of **you** playing on Buckshot are fine.

## Recruiting

Recruiting for another server is a one-way ticket. Ban. No appeal. Public, DMs, in the city, in a ticket — same line.

Your Buckshot Discord profile should not advertise other servers. Your main Discord “about me” is yours — the profile **in this Discord** is not a billboard.

## Discord clients

Do not use BetterDiscord or other modified Discord clients in Buckshot Discord. Official app only. These violate Discord’s Terms of Service and can get your account flagged.

## Copyright music

Do not play copyrighted music in the city. Streamers can eat a strike for it. Protect them.

## Bans

Staff have the final say. The wiki is the guide, not a contract you litigate. If you are slicing words to edge around a rule, we will not entertain it. The ban is whatever staff decide it is.

## Warning points

Breaks stack as warning points. Enough points and the city handles it automatically: priority gone, ban tiers, then a perm. Staff cannot waive those thresholds.

That does not replace staff’s last word. They can still add more, and they still decide anything the ladder does not cover.

## Ban appeals

Open a ban appeal ticket. Do not DM the staffer who banned you — they will not handle the appeal.

Do not litigate, twist, or stir the ban in public. That includes Discord, the city, comments, and stream talk.

## Ban evasion

Do not. A second account during a ban is a perm on both. Do not use an alt to watch Discord or the city, or to dodge social fallout. If you have another Discord or FiveM account, tell staff.

## Off-server conduct

Staff will not referee your personal fight. They can still remove you if what you do off the city threatens people here.

## Tickets

How to file a scene, what clip to attach, and player IDs live on [Footage](/rules/footage).

Open the right ticket type. Stay civil. Hostile tickets get closed. If you open one, watch it.

Keep it in the ticket. Feelings stay out of the process. The point is a record: who said what, who handled it, and that it got handled. People stay accountable. If a staffer needs to step off a ticket, they can — that is why it is not a DM.

Do not DM staff to skip the queue. The ticket system is how this stays manageable. Going around it can get you punished.

A ticket with no activity for **24 hours** gets closed. Staff will not explain why. Do not camp a ticket. It takes time out of someone’s day, and there is no reason to hold that if you are not in it.

Misusing tickets on a loop, or arguing with staff, is a ban from **Discord and the city**.

Staff will not tell you what they did to the other player. Ticket contents stay in the ticket.

## Honesty with staff

Tell the truth. Fake clips, fake refunds, and misleading tickets make it worse. Do not argue a ruling in public — staff complaint ticket.

Ideas for the city go on [Feedback](/rules/feedback). Broken scripts go on [Bug reports](/rules/bug-reports).

## Age

Buckshot is **18+** out of character. Characters are 18+ too — see [Character](/rules/character).

## Account and household

Your account is your problem. Shared logins still land on you. A ban on someone in the same household can land on you too.

## Intoxicated play

If you are too drunk or high to follow the rules, staff can remove you until you are sober. Breaks still count as if you were sober.

## Getting someone else to break a rule

If you put another player up to it, you are in it.

<Callout type="warning">
  No intent to roleplay is a removal. If you are here to shoot people and leave, you will not stay.
</Callout>
