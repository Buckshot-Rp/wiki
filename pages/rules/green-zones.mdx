# Green zones

No crime, no robberies, no kidnappings. Do not drag a fight in. Do not cuff someone and walk them out to finish it. Do not camp the doors and jump people as they leave. Do not block exits. Do not hide here to end RP.

A scene that **started** outside can finish if it spills in — keep it real, then leave.

## Hospitals

All hospitals. Do not treat one as a safe zone to cool off after a crime. EMS on scene is not a hostage — see [EMS](/rules/robbery#ems).

## PDs

All police stations. No hostage deals at a station. You will get shot. If someone is in custody inside the station, you do not raid it to free them. Negotiate from outside.

## Courthouses

All courthouses. Same line as PDs. Low-effort breakouts and shootouts with no story are fail RP.

## Car dealerships

All vehicle dealerships. Do not rob the lot, snatch cars off it, or start a scene on the floor.

## Events

Weddings, parties, block parties, concerts, debates, funerals, and other city events follow the same line. Do not jump them until they are over. A serious story that has to hit the event needs staff first. Take the rest of hostile RP somewhere else.
