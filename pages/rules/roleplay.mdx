# Roleplay

RP first. Guns last. Finish the scene, then ticket. Do not go OOC to rulesplain or to kill a scene.

Stay in character. Play the scene in front of you. Reciprocate when someone offers you RP — grinding is not an excuse. Violence happens here. If you are the one getting shot, robbed, or chased, play it. What happens to your character is not happening to you.

If you are committing crime and PD shows, give them something to work with. You can run. You still have to play the scene. Lawful orders live under [Low-effort RP](#low-effort-rp).

## /me, /do, tags

Use `/me` for things you cannot show: smell of alcohol, a round in the shoulder. `/do` for an action. A tag for a visible injury (cast on the arm). Not “ABC did it to you,” not spam, not a secret message.

If a `/me` is an inner thought (“thinks about killing Tom”), you do not know the thought. You might see they look angry. Do not read `/me`s out loud unless it is appearance (“missing an arm”). Ignore joke `/me`s like “is looking into your soul.”

Cooperate with PD / EMS inspect `/me`s. Answer with a truthful injury `/me`. Same if they check your radio.

Do not use `/me` to force an outcome on someone else. They need a way to answer. `/me slits throat and walks away` is out. Do not chain tie, loot, and shoot in one line.

## Illegal bragging

Do not spam socials with drugs, guns, or bodies. A rare in-character post is fine — expect PD to look into it.

City Instagram, Twitter, and the rest stay in character. Anyone in the city can use a post as in-character info — including PD. Threats or recruiting for crime on those apps can get a police follow-up. Lock your accounts. Do not ask for drug spots or job how-tos in OOC.

City socials stay in character. No IRL gore, nudity, or slurs — same line as [NSFW](/rules/community#nsfw).

## Selling hideouts

You can sell drugs. You cannot sell or buy the **location** of a grow, lab, or stash. Those get found through RP.

## Valuable locations

Do not hand a new player the drug map the minute they join. They can find it through RP like everyone else.

Spots that hold value — drug locations, crafting tables, and the rest — are worth something. Treat them that way. Do not broadcast them, do not gift them to a fresh spawn, do not treat them like public furniture.

Locations will move. If a spot is saturated, staff can change it so the economy stays balanced. Do not assume last month’s pin is still good.

Do not camp exits to illicit spots — drug components, processors, laundering, and the rest — to rob people coming or going. These are not safe zones. If RP in the area naturally escalates between people already involved, that is different. Sitting there to farm passersby is out.

## Low-effort RP

Put work in. Do not default to shooting, running from a ticket, or bikes on every robbery. You do not win every scene.

No empty shootouts. Do not wreck a business for fun. Do not hide in a [green zone](/rules/green-zones) after a serious crime to cool off or escape — see [Green zones](/rules/green-zones). Do not pause a gunfight to take photos or post. Leave. Fear getting bagged.

No fear of PD is low effort. If PD gives a lawful order, follow it unless you have a real RP reason not to — and then live with taser, cuffs, or rounds. Two cops on a scene does not mean you ignore them. Do not use a player-count or job-count display to decide whether to comply. Stealing cop cars on a loop, the same ocean getaway, the same ambush corner, and taser spam are the same bucket.

Going over **5** is [Group size](/rules/combat#group-size). Scripted scenes (a pre-written play you force on the city) are out. Play it in character.

Do not inject yourself into a PD stop, a kidnapping, or any scene you are not already in.

Your head popping in custody is not an escape. Turn yourself back in.

## Voice ID

Masked, clothes you have never seen, and they actually tried a different voice: you do not know who it is.

No mask, hair and tattoos showing, same crime fit every heist, no effort on the voice: you can suspect them. Catching them and confirming it still beats a guess.

## Breaking character

Stay in character. Find in-world words instead of keyboard, monitor, PC. If you have to step away, excuse it in character.

Do not call staff gods or government. Do not call your other character a cousin. Do not talk about recording, streaming, or “bodycam” unless the character has a camera in hand. Do not call a ban a deportation or a vacation. Do not say “in my head” and then talk about your real life. Do not mix OOC feelings into IC.

## OOC

OOC is for being stuck in an animation or a car, or for a real problem in the scene. It is not for drug locations, job tutorials, or arguing rules.

Do not PM staff. [Ticket](/rules/community#tickets) it.

## Body camping

After a kill, leave. Do not camp the body so EMS cannot work. If you drop an officer, other units are coming — your job is to leave. Same for blocking civs or other criminals who are trying to help.

No mag-dumping a downed body. No disgusting stuff to a corpse.

## Cop baiting

Do not do dumb things just to pull PD. Burnouts in front of a cop, clipping a scene you are not in, injecting yourself into a cop call. Ban.

## Vigilante RP

Do not play cop. Call PD. No sniper fantasy.

## Emergency services

Do not wear PD / EMS / DOJ clothes or peds. Do not steal emergency or military vehicles with no RP (planes, helicopters, tanks, jets, cargobobs). Helicopters are government response. Do not steal them. If you take a government car with a real RP reason, do not change wheels, neons, or anything else they cannot easily undo.

Do not fake judge work: gun licenses, name changes, expungements.

EMS rules — hostage, rob, kill — live under [Robbery](/rules/robbery#ems) and [Hostages and kidnapping](/rules/combat#hostages-and-kidnapping).

## Terrorism RP

No. Explosives exist; they need real RP, not a terror scene.

## Sexual RP

Not allowed. Ban. That includes ERP, sexual scenes, comments about someone’s body or genitals, and rape RP even if everyone “agreed.”

## Graphic content

Anything past normal GTA violence needs a yes from everyone who can see or hear it before it starts. Extended torture or dismemberment is that list. The victim must be able to keep playing afterward with functional body parts if they want — deformities from the scene can stay if they choose.

If someone says no, stop. Do not talk them into it.

Never, with or without a yes:

- Suicide, self-harm, or mental-health crisis RP
- Cancer, terminal illness, or life-threatening disease RP
- Real-world mass casualty, school shootings, or pandemic RP
- Pregnancy complications, abortion, or miscarriage RP
- Rape or sexual assault RP — see [Sexual RP](#sexual-rp)

## Fail RP

Unrealistic actions. Passing items through thick jail glass. Taking someone to a revive point or hospital so you can rob, torture, or kill them again.

If you already killed them for a reason, that reason is done. Do not hunt them again for it. Keep your distance.

Do not hold someone at gunpoint and force a menu they cannot actually open (bank NPC, gun shop, drug NPC).

Harming someone’s pet needs a real RP reason and is not a joke kill.

If you openly wreck the world for other people with low-quality RP or a bit that exists to annoy, staff can remove you. Win mentality, rushing every script, grinding with no scene, and a shooter-only approach are that bucket. You have to bring something to the RP.

Body dumps are [Combat](/rules/combat#bodies). Driving into the ocean to dump a car is [unrealistic driving](/rules/combat#unrealistic-driving).

## Metagaming

Your character only knows what they learned in RP. Discord and streams do not count. Do not mix memories across alts.

Using the info sideways still counts. If you would not have taken the action without it, it is meta.

Clout chasing is meta. Do not treat a streamer special with no in-character reason.

Streamers do not use their own chat for an in-character edge: fortifying before a raid, traveling for a scene chat asked for, or pinging someone because chat said so.

Meta chats are out. Streamers can poke their own Discord for a marketing beat. Living in the meta discussion is not.

If you are restrained or your radio / phone is gone, you are off every other channel. Proximity only. That includes a panic button they told you not to touch — see [Fear RP](/rules/character#fear-rp).

## Informing

Snitching is allowed if it is gradual and actually roleplayed. Do not dump a whole criminal encyclopedia on PD in one sitting.

Do not write or share criminal how-to docs — wikis, guides, private Discords, stream chats. That knowledge is taught in character.

Do not pull a stranger into crime with no thought that they might be undercover. Trust first.

## Powergaming

Do not bend mechanics to win the RP: jump spam, fake your injuries, radio with hands up or cuffed, hide a drawn gun in an emote, emote to run faster, tackle in a gunfight.

Leave the other player a way to answer, even in a robbery, a kidnapping, or a killing. Consequences of **their** choices are not powergaming.

Force-carry or cuff only if they are tackled, tased, hands up, or surrendered. That includes PD.

Civilians and criminals do not wear combat helmets. No bulletproof windows, louvers, body panels, or guns on the car. Armor and bulletproof **tires** through a mechanic are fine.

Play vehicle damage. A head-on into a pole totals it. Do not keep a 100 mph city cruise after eating walls. Stashing a car mid-chase is [unrealistic driving](/rules/combat#unrealistic-driving).

### Fifteen-minute rule

For **15 minutes** after a crime or hostile scene, you cannot stash loot, swap vehicles, change clothes to dodge ID, enter housing to evade anyone, run to a teleport to escape custody, or repair and keep using a hot vehicle as if nothing happened. Crafting illegal product inside player housing is out — MLOs and scripted spots are where that work lives.

Mid-chase garage stashes are [unrealistic driving](/rules/combat#unrealistic-driving). If you strip clothes to dodge ID, RP it (skybox or somewhere sensible).

You still cannot log out for **10 minutes** after a crime — see [Combat logging](/rules/server#combat-logging). The fifteen-minute line is separate: it covers stashes, swaps, and ID dodges even if you stay in the city.

## Whitelisted jobs

No crime on duty. Do not clock in just for the paycheck and ignore the work.

Do not abuse job tools:

- Mechanics putting free parts on their own cars
- Housing staff giving themselves a free house
- Unjailing or unbedding friends
- Using Discord logs or staff channels for in-character info (plates and the rest)

Cops do not bury evidence for friends, sell it, move it wrong, or play corrupt cop unless that is a staff-approved story. DOJ and EMS do not use MDT for personal dirt outside the job.

Government jobs have power. Offer counterplay. Using the job to hunt one group or person with no real RP is fail RP.

Do not sabotage your department because you are mad OOC. Ticket staff. In-character complaints and internal drama are fine.

Corruption as a story is allowed. Large-scale criminal corruption means you eat the full consequence: jail, losing the job, and being barred from government on that character. False info on a subpoena or warrant is out. Selling job-store supplies off the job is exploiting.

If you think PD mistreated you in character, use in-character channels first (supervisor, complaint, lawyer). Ticket staff only if a **server** rule broke.
