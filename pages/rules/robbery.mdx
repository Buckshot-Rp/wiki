# Robbery

## Before you start

Set the scene **before** the robbery starts. Do not kick it off and add people, hostages, or backup mid-job. Everyone involved should be there from the start.

Hostages, fake hostages, and on-duty minimums live under [Hostages and kidnapping](/rules/combat#hostages-and-kidnapping).

## Lootboxing

Do not loot someone without giving them RP.

You can take what is **on them** after a real scene: guns, ammo, illegal stuff, stolen goods, cash, lockpicks, armor, tablets and drills that make sense. Take a weapon or valuables. Stripping food, water, and junk with no reason is griefing. No pocket wipe. No bank transfers, no forcing a car sale, no emptying a house.

Do not rob someone **because** they are downed. If they went down in a real scene, loot that makes RP sense is fine. After a shootout, loot time is on [Combat](/rules/combat#after-a-shootout).

## Fresh spawns

Do not rob a fresh spawn. Nobody wants to drop in and get pocket wiped.

If they steal from you, that is fair game. Still be decent about new players — give them a chance to start in the city. Hunting new players is a ban. Do not hand them drug spots or other [valuable locations](/rules/roleplay#valuable-locations) the second they spawn either.

## Stores and banks

Do not chain them. **45 minutes** between store hits. **2 hours** between banks. Fear getting bagged — lay low.

No super / hyper cars, and no [drift cars](/rules/server#drift-cars), on those jobs. Same [restart window](/rules/server#restarts) as everything else. Do not counter a store or heist that is already running.

If the job needs a hostage and PD does not show, wait **5 minutes** before you leave. One hostage is one demand. You can have more hostages. PD generally allows one demand per hostage.

Do not drive any vehicle into a robbable building (banks, jewelry, and the rest). Free passage is **one** getaway car on scene. Split later if you want. Hostages do not count toward the [group size](/rules/combat#group-size) of **5**.

When PD asks how many people are in the building, tell them the number. You do not have to tell them where.

Do not open fire on PD the second they show. That is [RDM](/rules/combat#random-deathmatch). Talk first.

Player-owned businesses are not a free robbery target. That is griefing. Do not go to a business to kidnap or rob workers, customers, or people leaving — including payback — unless the **business** started the hostile RP. Even then, keep it proportional. Gangs pressing every employee on sight is harassment, not turf.

Police can serve warrants and patrol businesses normally.

## Street robberies

Not because you are broke. You need high-quality RP **and** a reason. Keep talking after the hold-up. Silent robberies are a ban. Someone in the group should say why: you kept coming back after we told you this is not your spot.

Do not chain-rob an illegal spot with no story. Do not rob every person you see. Do not camp a job spawn to tax or rob people working — same line as [camping illicit spots](/rules/roleplay#valuable-locations).

If you get robbed, play the victim. Taunting because you think they cannot kill you is how you eat a bullet and still eat a ticket.

## Bad-faith hold-ups

Do not hit someone who is stuck in a menu or a transition and cannot answer. Elevators, housing doors, clothing stores, vehicle customs, ladders. They need a realistic chance to respond first.

## Same player

You cannot rob or kidnap the same player more than once in **2 hours**. If they comply, you do not then kill them. Rob **or** kill, not both.

## Law enforcement

Robbing a cop is not for money and not because they did their job. Trash talk is not enough. It needs a planned story where that cop went way past the job, and **admin+** has to approve it. Do not rob them while they are downed. Do not take PD-issued guns. Do not force them to drop equipment the game will not let you rob. Breaking this is **2 weeks to a month**.

Intercepting a PD transport needs a real plan (disguise, story, effort). A chase and a spray is not it. Officer kidnaps need minimum LEO on duty — see [Minimum on duty](/rules/combat#minimum-on-duty).

## EMS

See [Hostages and kidnapping](/rules/combat#hostages-and-kidnapping) — EMS is never a hostage.

Do not rob or kill EMS unless **they** started it with you. Showing up to treat someone, working a scene, or doing the job is not starting it. If they draw on you, rob you, or otherwise come at you first, you can answer that.

## Prison

Breakouts are a heist. No pulling people through visiting bars, sliding kits under the glass, throwing over the fence, or flying a helicopter in.

## Outside help on a heist

If anyone outside the crew is involved in a store or bank, tell PD on scene.

A getaway driver or a non-violent extra waiting outside is fine. A planned ambush to kill PD is not. Outside help stays passive. If the crew inside gets in a gunfight with PD, the outside team does not join that fight. Deadly force with extras is last resort after a real escape attempt failed. Planning the gunfight from the start is low-effort RP and bans the whole group.
