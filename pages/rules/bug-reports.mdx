# Bug reports

Open a ticket. Be specific. “It broke” does not help.

A video helps a lot if you can get one. Steps to make it happen again help even more: what you were doing, where you were, what you clicked, what you expected, what actually happened.

## Lost loot

A refund for lost items needs a **video clip**. No clip, no refund. Record the session — see [Footage](/rules/footage#always-record).

Logs exist. Staff will not dig through them for a vague “I lost something.” They are not expected, asked, or required to. Bring a clip and a time, or there is nothing to look at.

Items the city pulls on purpose are [Loot rollbacks](/rules/loot-rollbacks), not this.
