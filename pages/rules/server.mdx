# Server

## Microphone

You need a working, clear mic on **push-to-talk**. Open mic that bleeds into a scene will get you removed. Staff can remove you if you do not have a working mic.

## Real-world trading

Do not buy or sell in-game items, cash, or services for real money or off-server goods.

## Bugs and exploits

No hacks, cheats, third-party software, or using bugs for an edge. Find a bug, **ticket it** — [Bug reports](/rules/bug-reports). Keeping it and using it is exploiting. Cheaters are a ban — report them on [Footage](/rules/footage#cheaters), do not trash talk them in the city.

Out:

- Aimbot
- Tracers / bullet indicators / confetti hit markers
- Night packs and anything that strips bushes, trees, water, props, or weather
- Wager / footstep packs that make steps louder for PvP (siren and gun sound packs are fine)
- Blood splatter packs
- External crosshairs
- Stretched aspect ratios (4:3 and similar)
- Keybind emotes for an advantage
- Gun packs that add non-vanilla tints or swap the gun model
- Hiding guns in animations, moving while overweight, emoting through walls
- Animation cancel to move when you should not
- Using the animation menu to clip walls, get into locked or unreachable spots, or eavesdrop
- Using animations in a fight to mess with hit detection, dodge shots, or cancel a taser or tackle
- Macros that act faster than a person
- Gym or other scripts to double XP
- Turning in a job or boost while PD is chasing you

ReShade, ENB, and NVE are fine. QuantV is not (pure mode). Looks nicer: fine. Fight advantage: not.

## Combat logging

Do not disconnect during or right after a hostile scene to dodge the RP.

If you crash, tell staff or the people in the scene and come back. Pick up where you left off. If you were under arrest in transport, turn yourself back in to PD.

If you have to leave mid-scene, everyone in it has to agree. Start a scene you can finish.

After any crime or hostile scene, wait **10 minutes** before you log out — do not vanish the second the shooting stops. After a PD scene or gang fight, stay on that character **15 minutes** before you log or swap characters.

Stashes, garage swaps, and ID dodges for **15 minutes** after crime are on the [Fifteen-minute rule](/rules/roleplay#fifteen-minute-rule).

## Scamming

Do not scam people out of **vehicles** or **properties**.

## Third-party voice

In-game voice and radio only during a scene. Discord and the rest is [metagaming](/rules/roleplay#metagaming).

## Green zones

Hospitals, PDs, courthouses, and car dealerships. The list and how they work is on [Green zones](/rules/green-zones).

## Restarts

No new crime in the **15 minutes** before or after a restart. Finish what is running; do not start a heist or a war. If a scene was live when the server died, come back and finish it.

## Streams

Do not watch streams while you are in the city. Using stream info in character is [metagaming](/rules/roleplay#metagaming). Streaming yourself on Buckshot is fine.

Do not use a live stream to learn someone’s location, story, or scripts. Do not sit in their chat while you are in the city, and do not sit in it while you are fighting them.

If **you** stream Buckshot, do not show direct routes into protected spots — drug processors, black markets, lab entrances, and the rest. Showing the inside when you are already there is fine. Do not pin the map for the city.

## Radio

In-game radios work in water but not while you are fully submerged or actively swimming. Resurface, tread water, or climb out before you transmit.

## AFK

Whatever happens while you are AFK is on you. Do not kill, rob, or mess with someone who is clearly AFK.

No AFK farming (fishing, mining, and the rest). If you are gone more than **15 minutes**, log out. Staff can kick you.

## Drift cars

Not for PD chases, heists, or getaways. Abuse and the car can be pulled with no refund.

## Lost items

Refunds need video. See [Bug reports](/rules/bug-reports#lost-loot). City-wide item pulls are [Loot rollbacks](/rules/loot-rollbacks).
