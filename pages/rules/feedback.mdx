# Feedback

Always welcome — good or bad. If something in the city sucks, tell us so we can make it not suck. If you want a feature you think other people would enjoy too, say it.

Be specific. “Dumpster diving sucks” does not help. What about it — the animation, the timing, the payout, the location? Ideas for how it could be better are what we can actually build on.

This is not the last city you played. Putting Buckshot down because it is not that server is not feedback. If you saw something elsewhere that worked, we want to hear it. Bring the idea, not the comparison dunk.

Do not walk the city complaining to other players. That kills the vibe, it is not constructive, and it helps nobody. Feedback belongs in a **ticket**. We want to hear you. We will not let someone sour the whole city because they would rather complain than help build it. Keep that up and you are gone.

This is modified GTA. There is a ceiling. Keep asks realistic. If you would not expect another city to change how a trunk opens, or to add a VR headset, do not expect it here either.

Open a ticket. Do not DM staff or pull them aside in the city. How to behave in a ticket is on [Community](/rules/community#tickets). Something broken is a [bug report](/rules/bug-reports), not this page.

The point of this city is to be shaped by the people in it: keep the ones who are here, make room for new ones, and stay a chill place where voices get heard. Nobody is trying to silence you. Use the ticket. Do not ruin the fun for everyone else.
