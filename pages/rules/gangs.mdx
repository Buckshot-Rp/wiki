# Gangs

You do not have to register a gang. Colors and matching fits are optional.

Scene numbers for crime, chases, and mixed crews live under [Group size](/rules/combat#group-size). That **5** is the whole city, not a gang-only rule.

## Beef

Needs a reason a third party would buy. Both sides have to understand why it exists. You cannot force beef onto a group that has no stake in it.

Initiation is **in person**. A social post is not enough.

Personal beef stays personal. The rest of the gang does not pile on unless there is a real story reason — it should not feel like a numbers dump.

The RP has to stay high the whole way. Staff can stop or change a beef at any time. Wait at least **one hour** between one situation ending and the next starting. Give EMS and PD time to exist.

No IRL gang names.

Do not fly in just to wake up and shoot. Talk it out in the city.

## False flag

Do not wear another set’s colors, spray their tag, or paint your car their scheme to frame them for your crime. Rival sets can press that in RP. Staff shut it down hard when it becomes a pattern.

If you wear another gang’s custom fit or bandana in their turf without being in that set, they can treat it as false flagging.

## Drive-bys

Gang-on-gang only, in colors, after real escalation — not on civs or PD. You still need initiation — see [Random deathmatch](/rules/combat#random-deathmatch). Circle or slow-roll the block; shoot from the vehicle; leave. Do not stop, exit, and start a full firefight. The target set can track you down for retaliation if the story supports it.

## Civilian jobs

Do not claim, tax, or lock down garbage, recycling, mail, metal detecting, or other civ work. Those stay open.

Someone doing a legal job in your turf is not a reason to press them. Active war with **that** set, and they showed up on your block, is a reason. Do not camp a spawn and farm it.

If you work a public business, do not clock in while your gang is at war and put civs in the crossfire.

## Ocean dumps

Last resort. Rare. You cannot use a dump to force a perma. [Character kill](/rules/character#character-kill) is still their choice.

The dumpers explain why in character: you cannot walk around knowing this operation. The dumped character forgets major story tied to that group or work.

Valid: a cop who found the hideout or the plan; a member leaving who knows too much; a repeat spy.

Invalid dumps are punished hard, ban history or not. If staff toss the dump, the victim keeps the memories and cannot be dumped again for that.

The dumped player can still send PD to where they originally went down. Do not use the water to hide every piece of evidence.

## Keys and labs

If you get robbed of a lab key, staff will not replace it. RP that you lost it. Do not gatekeep product to your own set — sell to players. Do not hand a new player the whole map. See [Valuable locations](/rules/roleplay#valuable-locations).

## Gang wars

Rare. Last resort after a lot of RP, not a excuse to loot bodies. You still initiate. A war is not shoot-on-sight.

Terms include what is at stake and numbers (default still **5**). “Pay or die” money terms are out. If one side will not war, settle what you were pressing (turf, sprays, keys). A side that wants to yield gets to yield. You do not hold a gang hostage in a war.

If you want a break or an end, parley. Terms you agree are binding. Write them down somewhere your set can see.

Cooldown **14 days** before the same two gangs war again. Story still has to support it.

No war at apartments, hospitals, or government buildings. City events follow [green zones](/rules/green-zones). Third parties stay out. Alliances do not stack numbers past the cap.

When you are at war, be identifiable. Do not black out clothes and cars unless both sides agreed.

Take the L. Do not go OOC in the city. Do not DM staff because you lost. Ticket if a **rule** broke. Staff will not pick a side to protect a set.

[Record](/rules/footage). If the other side has a clip and you only have a story, the clip wins.
