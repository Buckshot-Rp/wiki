# Hey.

This is **Buckshot RP**. An adult roleplay city. Eighteen and up. English on Discord and in the city.

<IntroHero />

## Play

The point is a full ecosystem you can actually live in: play, stream, bring friends, and stay because there is something to do besides a gunfight. Jobs, crime, businesses, characters that last more than a night. Mechanics go deep on purpose. If a loop is shallow, we would rather hear it and change it than pretend it is finished.

Stream if you want. Bring people if they will respect the room. This is meant to be a chill place to play where voices get heard, not a lobby you pass through.

## Shape it

We run on what works and what does not. Something that looks good on paper and feels bad in a scene gets cut or rebuilt. Something the city actually uses gets more love. [Feedback](/rules/feedback) is how that happens — good or bad, specific, in a ticket. We read it. We talk it through. We do not owe every idea a yes, and we do not ship a VR headset because someone asked. We do owe you a city that is trying.

## Start here

Connecting means you have read the [Rules](/rules). They will not cover every situation. They exist so this stays safe and worth staying in.
